09 / GOOD TO KNOW

YOUR QUESTIONS.
ANSWERED.

Travel better when you know the details. Everything here is based on the official tour brochure.

Still have questions? Talk to us ↗
What are the tour dates and starting point?+

21–31 January 2027, covering 11 days and 10 nights. The advertised itinerary begins and ends in Ahmedabad.

Does ₹39,999 include my vehicle and fuel?+

No. The listed ₹39,999 price is per person on double sharing. Vehicle and fuel are excluded, along with 5% GST, lunch and other listed costs.

What are the booking and payment terms?+

The brochure lists a ₹5,000 booking amount, 50% payment by 30 December 2026 and the remaining balance by 18 January 2027. Confirm how these payments are applied with the organisers.

What is the cancellation policy?+

As printed: the booking amount is non-refundable; a 50% refund of the amount paid is stated for cancellation before 14 January; a 15% refund of the amount paid is stated for the last seven days. Because the booking amount and refund terms may overlap, confirm the exact applicable refund in writing before paying.

Can I rent a 4×4 vehicle?+

Yes. Optional self-drive rentals ex-Chandigarh are listed at ₹4,000/day for a Jimny and ₹4,500/day for a 3-door Thar. A security deposit, valid driving licence and Aadhaar card are required.

Will every village and viewpoint be accessible?+

Not necessarily. The brochure specifically makes certain visits subject to time, road access or conditions. Winter road closures and safety requirements can change the route or activities.

Are accommodation and meals included?+

Accommodation includes a mix of hotels, resorts, homestays and high-altitude camps / cottages. Breakfast and dinner are listed from Day 1 start to Day 10 finish; lunches are excluded.

Is there an age restriction?+

Yes. The brochure lists 18+ as mandatory. This is a remote, high-altitude winter expedition: consult a medical professional about personal fitness or altitude-related concerns before travelling.
